Enchiladas Rojas

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 12 chiles guajillos
  • 20ml of oil
  • 1/2 cup of onion
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 cup of cheese oaxaca

Instructions

  1. Remove stems and seed from chiles guajillos
  2. Soak chiles in warm water for 10 minutes
  3. Blend chiles with a little water until smooth
  4. Heat oil in a pan and saute onion
  5. Add the blended chiles to the pan and simmer for 10 minutes
  6. Dip tortillas in the sauce and layer in a baking dish
  7. Top with cheese and bake at 180°C for 20 minutes
